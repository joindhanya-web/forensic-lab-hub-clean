ABOUT THE PROJECT

Science meets investigation.

Forensic science applies scientific methods to the examination of evidence. It supports investigations by helping experts analyze materials, reconstruct events and present reliable findings.

This learning hub introduces key forensic fields and explains how laboratory methods contribute to the investigative process.

What happens in a forensic lab?

Evidence is carefully documented, collected, preserved, examined and interpreted. Every step matters—from protecting samples against contamination to maintaining a clear record of evidence handling.

INVESTIGATION SIMULATIONS

Case files.

Read the scenario, consider the evidence and explore a responsible investigative approach.

01
BURGLARY · TRACE EVIDENCE

The Broken Window

A property is found disturbed. A broken window and partial impressions are documented during the initial examination.

Explore investigation approach

Secure the scene, photograph the window and surrounding area, record observations, protect potential trace evidence and maintain documentation. An impression alone should not be treated as proof of identity.

02
LABORATORY · CHEMISTRY

The Unknown Substance

A sealed container holding an unknown substance is submitted to the laboratory for examination.

Explore investigation approach

Check packaging and labels, document the condition of the sample, follow laboratory safety procedures and use validated analytical methods. Conclusions should be based on test results.

03
DIGITAL · DEVICE ANALYSIS

The Digital Trail

A mobile device is recovered during an investigation and may contain relevant digital information.

Explore investigation approach

Document the device and its condition, preserve it using appropriate procedures, record authorized handling and examine relevant data while maintaining evidence integrity.

Core forensic principles.

Fundamental concepts that guide reliable forensic examination.

⚖️

Locard's Exchange Principle

Contact between people, objects or environments may result in a transfer of material.

🧤

Evidence Preservation

Careful collection, packaging, storage and documentation help protect evidence from contamination or loss.

📋

Chain of Custody

A continuous record documents who collected, handled, transferred and stored evidence.

🔬

Scientific Method

Observations, testing, analysis and conclusions should be systematic, transparent and supported by results.
